About AURA

What is this certificate?

AURA is a forensic certification system that evaluates the role of artificial intelligence in the creative process of an artwork. It does not judge artistic value — it documents creative origin so that galleries, collectors, institutions and artists can communicate transparently.

Each certificate is the result of a human audit conducted by a certified AURA auditor, following the AURA Motor Forense protocol v2.1. The result is permanent and verifiable on the Polygon blockchain, independently of AURA's infrastructure.

Aura Scale — six levels

The Aura Scale classifies the functional relationship between the artwork and AI — not as a percentage, but as a role. An analogue work with poor documentation can be CG-C; a fully AI-generated work with extensive evidence can be CG-A.

Aura 0 · Ancestral Origin
Entirely analog. No digital tools in the creative process.
Aura 1 · Manual Origin
Digital, but with deterministic tools only. No generative AI.
Aura 2 · AI-Augmented
AI assists in support tasks. Compositional authorship is entirely human.
Aura 3 · Co-Creation
AI intervenes partially. The artist retains full compositional control.
Aura 4 · Hybrid AI
The visual core is generative. The human role is art direction.
Aura 5 · Prompt-Based
Fully AI-generated. Artistic value lies in concept and selection.
Confidence Grade

The Confidence Grade reflects the strength of the evidence provided to support the classification. It is assessed independently of the Aura level.

A
CG-A · High Confidence
Backed by extensive, coherent and verifiable documentation (source files, process records, no forensic flags).
B
CG-B · Standard Confidence
Backed by sufficient evidence with no material contradictions.
C
CG-C · Limited Confidence
Assigned with limited evidence, though no contradictions were found. Subject to revision upon new evidence.
The audit process

Human audit, not algorithmic scoring

Every AURA certificate is the result of a human review. The AURA Motor Forense analyses source files, metadata, creation process records and declared workflow. The auditor may request additional evidence before issuing a final classification.

The classification is not based on how the artwork looks, but on documented evidence of how it was made. Style, aesthetics and artistic value are outside the scope of the audit.

Blockchain anchor

Once issued, the certificate is anchored on Polygon Mainnet. The anchor contains a SHA-256 fingerprint of the audit evidence, the Aura Level, the Confidence Grade and the ruleset version. No personal data is stored on the blockchain. The anchor makes the result permanently verifiable, independently of AURA's systems.
