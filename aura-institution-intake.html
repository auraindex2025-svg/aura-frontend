Provenance

Everything here is optional. It is recorded with the case and printed on the certificate as a custody statement.

This does not affect the Confidence Grade. The grade measures whether the artist's declaration is supported by evidence of how the work was made. An acquisition invoice, however impeccable, says nothing about that. It is worth recording for your own catalogue, and it tells an auditor who has held the work — but it cannot raise or lower a grade, and we would rather say so than let you assume otherwise.
